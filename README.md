## 🎨 Visual Identity & Design Balance

Engineered to the precise visual ratio:

* **60% — 3D Clean Professional Design**: Premium claymorphism with dual inner specular highlights and ambient soft shadows, tactile 3D buttons, rounded geometric components, layered white surfaces on porcelain, and clean editorial layouts.
* **20% — Cyberfunk Personality**: Monospace technical micro-labels, digital typography accents (JetBrains Mono), live availability beacon (`● AVAILABLE`), scanning beam animations, cobalt & violet accenting, and fine dot-grid canvas.
* **10% — Premium Animations**: 
  * Character-by-character typewriter hero animation with natural pacing and blinking cursor.
  * Real-time live IST date and clock (`31 OCT 2026 | 03:45:22 PM IST`).
  * Dynamic greeting adapting to local time (*Good Morning*, *Good Afternoon*, *Good Evening*).
  * Interactive 3D card tilt tracking desktop cursor movement on the OPERATOR_NODE card.
  * Floating isometric geometric shapes and smooth section transitions.
  * Full accessibility with `prefers-reduced-motion` guards.
* **10% — Expressive Maximalism**: Oversized display typography (Space Grotesk), giant section ghost numbers (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`, `05`), asymmetric layout compositions, and bold accent blocks.
